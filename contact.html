FAQ

Before you ask

How fast can you deliver?

A single poster or portrait edit is usually ready within 24–48 hours. Bigger sets and video projects take three to five days depending on the scope.

How many revisions do I get?

Two rounds are included in every project. After that I charge a small hourly rate, but it rarely comes to that if the brief is clear.

What do you need from me to start?

A short description of the goal, any reference images you like, your logo or brand colours if you have them, and the sizes you need.

Do you use AI to generate the artwork?

No. Everything is edited and composed by hand. I only use AI-free tools for masking and cleanup that ship inside Photoshop.

How do payments work?

Half up front, half on delivery. Bank transfer or e-wallet, whichever is easier for you.
