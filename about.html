About me

Self-taught editor,
full-time obsessive.

I'm Yudha Pradana, 21, from Halmahera Timur. I started editing in 2018 on a phone, mostly because I wanted my football edits to look better than everyone else's. That turned into graphic design, then typography, then video.

Most of my work lives between poster design and portrait retouching — heavy contrast, warm rim light, and type that carries the mood. I care about the small stuff: kerning, edge quality, how the light falls. Nothing here is generated; every frame was pushed by hand.

Right now I'm open for freelance work and always up for a project that lets me try something new.
